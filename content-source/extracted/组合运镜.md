__组合运镜__

文生图 \(Text\-to\-Image\) 

1. Medium shot, low angle, looking up at a heroic warrior holding a sword, dramatic sunset lighting, shallow depth of field, cinematic\.  
\(中景，仰视一位手持长剑的英雄战士，戏剧性的日落光线，浅景深，电影感。\)
2. Extreme close\-up on a weathered hand holding a rose, shallow depth of field, bokeh background, soft lighting, melancholic mood\.  
\(极度特写一只布满皱纹的手拿着一朵玫瑰，浅景深，背景虚化，柔和光线，忧郁氛围。\)
3. Dutch angle, wide shot of a futuristic city skyline at night, neon lights reflecting on wet streets, cyberpunk aesthetic, deep depth of field\.  
\(荷兰角，未来都市夜景广角镜头，霓虹灯在湿漉漉的街道上反射，赛博朋克美学，深景深。\)
4. Overhead shot of a chessboard, one piece being moved by an unseen hand, minimalist composition, dramatic shadow\.  
\(俯视镜头拍摄棋盘，一只无形的手正在移动棋子，极简构图，戏剧性阴影。\)
5. Long shot, back view of a lone figure standing on a cliff edge overlooking a vast ocean, stormy clouds, high winds \(suggested by hair/clothing\), epic scale\.  
\(远景，一个孤独的身影背对镜头站在悬崖边眺望浩瀚海洋，暴风云，强风（通过头发/衣物暗示），史诗感。\)

图生视频 \(Image\-to\-Video\)  \(假设输入图片是示例描述的画面\)

1. __\(输入：英雄战士中景仰视图\)__ Slow push\-in on the warrior's determined face, wind blowing through his hair\.  
\(缓慢推进到战士坚毅的脸部，风吹动他的头发。\)
2. __\(输入：手拿玫瑰特写\)__ Slow pull\-out to reveal an old woman sitting alone in a dimly lit room, looking at the rose with tears in her eyes\.  
\(缓慢拉出，展现出一位老妇人独自坐在昏暗房间里，眼含泪水看着玫瑰。\)
3. __\(输入：未来城市广角荷兰角图\)__ Camera slowly tilts up the towering skyscrapers, flying cars zooming by in the foreground\.  
\(镜头缓缓上摇高耸的摩天大楼，飞行汽车在前景疾驰而过。\)
4. __\(输入：俯视棋盘图\)__ Close\-up on the hand placing the chess piece decisively, then cut to \(or pan to\) the opponent's reaction shot \(if generating multi\-shot might需要更复杂提示或分步\)\.  
\(特写手果断放下棋子的瞬间，然后切到（或摇到）对手的反应镜头 \- 生成多镜头可能更复杂。\)
5. __\(输入：悬崖边人物远景背影图\)__ Camera slowly orbits around the figure, revealing the stormy sea crashing against the cliffs below, dramatic music swell\.  
\(镜头缓缓环绕人物移动，展现出下方波涛汹涌的大海拍打悬崖，戏剧性音乐渐强。\)
